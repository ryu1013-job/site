Write posts as MDX files in this directory. Export a `meta` object, then write the body in markdown.

## Frontmatter shape

- `title` — required
- `date` — required, `YYYY/MM/DD`
- `subTitle` — optional
- `category` — required, one or more of `tech` | `product` | `design` | `work` | `talk` | `yuru`
- `draft` — optional; when `true`, the post is omitted from the index, feed, and sitemap

## Body

Supports GFM (tables, task lists, strikethrough) and Japanese-friendly `**bold**`.

```md
A short paragraph with **emphasis**.

- list item
```
